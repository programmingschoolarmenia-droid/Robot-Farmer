# Calendar, seasons, weather, luck
Code: `js/engine/calendar.js`, `js/ui/gameScreen.js` (calendar chip, TV), `settings.json` → `calendar`. Use `makeGame({ clock })`.

- [ ] Each hour: spring :00, summer :15, autumn :30, winter :45, and the same for every player.
- [ ] Rain waters the whole farm, and lightning ×3. Nothing dies.
- [ ] 12 daily lucks change at midnight (Yerevan time), also with a device clock in another timezone or set wrong.
- [ ] Season change while a program runs: no freeze, and one toast.
- [ ] The greenhouse glass keeps the favourite season, with no weather inside.
- [ ] The TV shows the right season, weather and luck at all sizes.
