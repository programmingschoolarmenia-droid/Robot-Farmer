# Village
Code: `js/engine/village.js`, `js/ui/village.js`, `config/village.json`, `assets/village/`.

- [ ] Opens after «Plant». Giving items little by little; repair when complete, story pages, and `after` order respected.
- [ ] 3 requests a day (×3 amounts, `share` 6%), stars paid, the bonus for all 3. New requests at midnight.
- [ ] The star shop works only once the whole village stands.
- [ ] Machines: `karucel`, `patrastel`, `hasunE`, `havaqel`, `herkel`; dishes with `utel`; glass with `apakepatel`.
- [ ] Pictures and ruins line up with the background at all 3 sizes. On a portrait phone only the chosen building shows its name.
- [ ] Buttons work on the first click while a farm program runs.
